**What Mirra is.** Mirra is a daily-reflection app that lets you write about your
day and, if you choose, connect wearables and other accounts so your own patterns
become visible to you. It is offered as-is while in development.

**Not medical advice.** Mirra is not a medical device and does not diagnose,
treat, or prevent any condition. Insights it shows are observations about the
data you gave it, not clinical guidance. Talk to a qualified professional about
health decisions.

**Your account.** You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details private.
Do not use someone else's account, and do not connect an account you do not own.

**Your data stays yours.** You decide which sources to connect and can
disconnect any of them at any time. You can ask us to delete your account and
everything attached to it. How data is handled is described in the Privacy
Policy, which is part of these terms.

**Connected services.** When you connect a third-party account (Oura, Whoop,
Fitbit, Spotify), that provider's own terms and privacy policy govern their
side of the connection. We only request the read permissions the app needs.

**Availability.** Mirra is under active development. Features may change,
break, or be removed, and we may pause the service without notice.

**Changes.** We will update these terms as the product changes, and the date at
the top will say when.
