# Mirra Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

**What we collect:** Your daily reflections (text, mood rating), and 
optionally Oura Ring health data (sleep, readiness, activity, HRV) if 
you connect your Oura account.

**Where it's stored:** Supabase (encrypted at rest). Your data is 
scoped to your account; no other user can read it.

**Third parties:** Reflection text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude 
API to generate AI insights you request. Anthropic's privacy policy 
applies to that processing.

**Deletion:** Email mirra@yourdomain.com to delete your account and 
all associated data, or disconnect Oura at any time from Settings.

**Contact:** mirra@yourdomain.com
