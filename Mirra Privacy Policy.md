# Mirra Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

**What we collect:** Your daily reflections (text, mood rating), and — only for
the sources you choose to connect — health and activity data from those
accounts. Today those are Oura (sleep, readiness, activity, HRV), Whoop
(recovery, sleep, day strain), Fitbit (sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, steps)
and Spotify (listening activity). Connecting a source is optional, and
disconnecting it stops any further collection.

**Where it's stored:** Supabase (encrypted at rest). Your data is scoped to your
account by row-level security; no other user can read it. Access tokens for
connected sources are encrypted before they are stored.

**Third parties:** Reflection text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to
generate AI insights you request. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that
processing. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

**Deletion:** Email mirra@yourdomain.com to delete your account and all
associated data, or disconnect any source at any time from the Connections page.

**Contact:** mirra@yourdomain.com
